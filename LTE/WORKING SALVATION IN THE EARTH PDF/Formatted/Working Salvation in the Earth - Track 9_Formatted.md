Healing power is free. That's why as the Savior one of the greatest signs of salvation is healing. Forget not all His benefits—who daily loaded you. He daily loaded us with benefit. Bless the Lord, O my soul, and forget not all His benefits. I'm not regretting being saved. I'm happy. I'm joyful that I'm born again. I'm not putting my head down and saying if not for Jesus Christ now the kind of life I… there's no kind of life. I don't regret being saved. I'm not wasting away that I know Jesus. No—I love it that I know Jesus. I love it that He knows me. I love it that He's my Father. I can always count on Him. Friends may forget me. Friends may betray me. But I have a Friend who does not forget—who cannot leave me—who will not leave me—that even in the bad day that I've said everybody should leave me He still will not leave me. I will never leave you nor forsake you—so that you may boldly say God is helping me. God is helping me. He's the helper of my life. What a Savior we have. That's…
