## Christ Died for the Whole World — Not All Are Saved

Christ died for the whole world? Yes. Is the whole world saved? Of course not. It takes a man to believe in the Lord Jesus Christ. Then he is saved. Romans 10:8. What saith it? The word is nigh thee, even in thy mouth, and in thy heart: that is, the word of faith, which we preach.

> **Romans 10:8–10**
> But what saith it? The word is nigh thee, even in thy mouth, and in thy heart: that is, the word of faith, which we preach; that if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved. For with the heart man believeth unto righteousness; and with the mouth confession is made unto salvation.

If thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised Him from the dead, thou shalt be saved. For with the heart man believeth unto righteousness; and with the mouth confession is made unto salvation. So the efficacy of the death of Jesus is by faith in the blood. Is by faith in God. Is by faith in what He has done in His Son. He died for the whole world. But the whole world is not saved. That’s why we go to the world with the gospel of Jesus Christ. To the intent that they receive the gospel, they believe, and then they are saved.
